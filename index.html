What It Took

The Requirements

Reaching the Mitchell Award means clearing every one of these gates — no shortcuts, no partial credit.

Leadership Exam

A comprehensive, closed-book exam on Learn to Lead, Volume 2 — passed at 80% or higher.

Aerospace Exam

A closed-book exam on Aerospace Dimensions — every module, cyber included — also at 80% or higher.

Physical Fitness

Met the Healthy Fitness Zone on the Cadet Physical Fitness Test under the run-plus-two-of-three standard.

Encampment

Graduated a week-long encampment — long days, high standards, and full immersion in the core values.

Leadership in Action

Served in flight and staff roles, coached junior cadets, and led drill and public speaking — proven, not just tested.

Character & Commitment

Months of consistent attendance, character development, and active service to the squadron through it all.

15%

Only about 15 percent of all Civil Air Patrol cadets ever earn the General Billy Mitchell Award. YOU are now one of them.
